# Reliable pair allocation for the frozen M1 pilot

The existing `mixlab.evaluation.study freeze` first attempts the original seeded, hash-ordered, harmonically stratified greedy assignment. This is retained exactly for study selections that were already feasible. Previously a legal library could fail spuriously: with five eligible evaluation sources and a maximum of two uses per source, some seeds greedily selected four edges then stranded the fifth, even though a five-edge cycle is feasible.

The product now falls back **only after that greedy assignment fails** to a deterministic, bounded search of the same source-bound candidate edges. It prioritizes the original approximately half-compatible / half-contrasting-or-unknown selection policy, but backtracks across choices to avoid exhausting the wrong recordings. No selected pair is duplicated, source partitions are unchanged, no source exceeds the frozen reuse limit, and the resulting candidate selection and preselected transition family remain bound to the recorded seed and study SHA. The source track, BPM and permissions checks are unchanged.

For at most sixteen evaluation tracks, up to 40,000 distinct candidate subsets may be examined; larger sets use a tighter 1,500-state limit to avoid monopolizing the shared VPS. If the search limit is exhausted the CLI reports an explicit bounded-search error rather than incorrectly claiming the inputs are mathematically impossible. A genuinely infeasible study remains rejected. This is an allocation algorithm, **not** an automatic measurement of music quality, key accuracy, copyright rights or preference.

Direct product evidence on self-hosted VPS: 256 seeds of five synthetic candidate metadata records each yielded five distinct, SHA-bound pairs with at most two uses per source, including multiple previously failing seeds; independently impossible four-source/five-pair input was refused. A full real PCM pipeline with seed 2 (previously rejected) completed candidate import, explicit unknown-key review, frozen study, user-cue worksheet, ten actually rendered matched WAVs and `verify_pack` / `verify_study_pack`. Those manual decisions were simulated for generated audio only, not a real human study. No additional QA suites were created or manually executed.
